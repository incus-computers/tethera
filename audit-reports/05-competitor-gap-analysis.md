# Competitor gap analysis

Research date: 2026-11-06. Public read-only pages/search-index content only. No competitor accounts, checkouts, scans or purchases. Full = feature clearly surfaced in inspected material, not audited operational quality; Partial = limited implementation or evidence; Missing = absent from the audited repository inventory; Not verified = insufficient/inaccessible evidence; N/A = not applicable.

Repository evidence comes from source inspection; competitor evidence comes from the specific public pages below. A generic “PC builder” claim does not establish automated compatibility, stock, price integrity or checkout quality.

## Public sources and limits

- **Enter Komputer:** public simulation previews show configurable component selections, socket/platform context and specification/warranty information in product titles. This verifies a simulation/specification capability, not safe checkout or complete compatibility coverage. [Public simulation preview](https://www.enterkomputer.com/simulasi/preview/TBL3IPRF), [simulation page](https://www.enterkomputer.com/simulasi).
- **Nano Komputer:** public homepage displays component categories, ready-PC products, detailed hardware specification text, IDR prices, official-warranty messaging and a dedicated RMA contact. Build Your Own is publicly listed, but its interactive rules and completed checkout were not exercised. [Homepage](https://nanokomputer.com/), [Build Your Own collection](https://nanokomputer.com/collections/build-your-own). The www host initially failed, whereas the canonical non-www page was available through search.
- **COC Komputer:** cockomputer.com currently presents technology/editorial content and shop/marketplace contact information. This is insufficient to verify the intended retailer's current transactional capabilities or identity/ownership of the domain. All transactional capabilities below remain Not verified. [Observed public page](https://www.cockomputer.com/).
- **Pemmz:** both attempted host variants were inaccessible through the browser tool (403 on canonical pemmz.com). Capabilities are Not verified, not Missing. [Attempted homepage](https://pemmz.com/).
- **Tokopedia/Shopee:** optional broader UX benchmarks were not inspected. No claimed parity or feature ratings are inferred.

## Business-value priorities

| Gap / improvement | Value and rationale | Complexity | Priority / dependencies |
|---|---|---|---|
| Real paid/stock/shipping correctness | Protects cash and fulfilment for every order; outweighs parity features | XL | P0; identity, schema and transaction work |
| Server-enforced variant/build compatibility | Prevents underpriced/incompatible systems and expensive support | L | P0 financial authority, then P1 compatibility breadth |
| Clear Bahasa Indonesia warranty/returns/support | Reduces purchase uncertainty for expensive hardware; visible competitor warranty information supports expectation | M | P1; operational policy and verified contact ownership |
| Accurate per-SKU/package metadata and insured shipping | Reduces quote disputes/damage exposure for cases, monitors and complete PCs | L | P1; catalog dimensions, packing and Biteship recovery |
| Reliable cart/account/order status across devices | Improves conversion and after-sales trust | L | P1; real sessions and durable storage |
| Serial-number intake/dispatch/RMA linkage | High operational value for high-value electronics, warranty proof and restock decisions | L | P2; inventory/returns lifecycle |
| Complete filterable specifications | Helps enthusiasts choose parts and improves discovery | M–L | P2; controlled taxonomy/catalog quality |
| Reproducible saved/shared builds and quotations | Useful for assisted/B2B purchases; formal acceptance optional | M–L | P2/P3; authoritative pricing/expiry and ownership |
| Component comparison | Useful for evaluation but requires high-quality comparable specifications | M | P3 after catalog quality; no verified competitor parity requirement |
| Bundles/prebuilt merchandising | Useful existing foundation; trusted BOM price/stock must precede new campaigns | M | P2; stock, promotions and snapshot accuracy |
| Advanced benchmark/configurator polish | Differentiation for enthusiasts/workstations | L–XL | P3; correctness, performance and support capacity first |

Secure payments, ownership, fulfilment and honest availability are critical business requirements independent of competitor implementations. Comparison, advanced quotations and rich benchmarking are optional enhancements; do not block transactional repairs on them.
